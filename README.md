## What is Dinosaur?

Dinosaur is a spatial browser for creatives — built on Electron, designed from the ground up for people who think visually and work across multiple contexts simultaneously.

Standard browsers treat every tab as an isolated destination. Dinosaur treats your browser as a *workspace*. Your research, moodboards, saved links, local files, and collaborators all live in one unified window. No context switching. No losing the thread.

It is free, requires no account, and runs entirely on your machine.

---

## Core Features

### Spatial Moodboard Canvas
An infinite, freeform canvas lives inside your browser. Drag web content, screenshots, and local files onto it. Connect cards. Annotate. Build visual research boards the way your mind actually works — not the way folders do.

### Stash
Save anything from any page to named collections without interrupting your flow. Pull it back when you need it. Your stash persists across sessions and is always one click away.

### Co-browse
Share your exact browser state with collaborators in real time. Named cursors, live navigation sync, view-only or full-control modes. Proximity without a meeting link.

### Local File Access
Your file system lives in the sidebar. Browse folders, preview assets, and drag files directly onto your canvas — without leaving the browser or opening a separate application.

### Screenshot & Clip
Capture any region, viewport, or full page and send it to your canvas instantly. One keystroke from browsing to building.

### Tab Workspaces
Organize tabs into named workspaces. Switch contexts cleanly. Your tabs are not a list — they are a workspace.

## Getting Started

1. Download `Dinosaur-Setup-1.0.0-beta.1.exe` from [Releases](../../releases)
2. Run the installer — no admin rights required for per-user install
3. Launch Dinosaur and start browsing

Minimum: Windows 10 · 4 GB RAM · 500 MB disk

## Philosophy

> *Not just a browser. A spatial workspace built for the way creatives actually think.*

Every decision in Dinosaur prioritizes depth over breadth. We are not building a faster Chrome. We are building a different kind of tool — one that respects the non-linear, visually-driven way that creatives actually work.

Dinosaur merges browsing, research, moodboarding, and local files into a single unified window so you never lose the thread of an idea.
