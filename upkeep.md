# Dinosaur Community Upkeep Guide

Welcome to the Dinosaur community repository! While the Dinosaur Browser itself is closed-source, this GitHub space is our public hub for official releases, product updates, and most importantly—hearing from you. This guide explains how to participate by writing effective issues and discussions to help shape the future of this spatial workspace.

## Overview

Dinosaur thrives on thoughtful feedback from creatives—designers, writers, filmmakers, and explorers. We use two main channels here:

- **Issues** — Bug reports and confirmed problems
- **Discussions** — Feature ideas, workflows, questions, and community chat

---

## Writing Great Issues (Bug Reports)

An issue is where we track bugs—things that don't work as intended.

### Before You Submit

1. **Search first** — Check if someone already reported this. Duplicate issues clutter our backlog.
2. **Test on latest** — Make sure you're on the newest version of Dinosaur.
3. **Isolate the problem** — Can you reproduce it consistently? What steps lead to it?

### Issue Template

**Title:** One sentence describing what's broken.
- ✅ Good: "Canvas cards don't save position when switching tabs"
- ❌ Bad: "bug" or "it's broken"

**Description:**

```
**What happened?**
Clear description of the problem. What were you trying to do?

**Steps to reproduce:**
1. Open Dinosaur
2. Create a card on the canvas
3. Switch to another tab
4. Switch back to canvas

**Expected behavior:**
Card should remain in the same position.

**Actual behavior:**
Card resets to center of canvas.

**Screenshots/Video:**
If it's visual, a screenshot helps a lot.

**Environment:**
- Dinosaur version: 1.0.0-beta.1
- Windows 10 or 11?
- Any extensions or special setup?

**Additional context:**
Happens on both dark and light theme, but only with large images.
```

### Good Issue Practices

- **Be specific** — "The app is slow" is less useful than "Opening a PDF with 500 pages takes 45 seconds"
- **One problem per issue** — Don't combine 3 unrelated bugs
- **Include steps** — Help us reproduce it
- **Attach media** — A 5-second video > 1000 words
- **Be respectful** — Remember, real people built this, and real people will fix it

## Writing Great Discussions (Ideas & Questions)

Discussions are for exploring ideas, asking questions, and getting feedback before something becomes a formal feature request.

### Good Discussion Topics

- "Has anyone figured out how to organize 500+ images on canvas?" (Question)
- "I'm thinking about a feature where cards could be grouped into folders" (Idea)
- "What's your workflow for capturing inspiration from multiple sites?" (Poll/Chat)
- "Should Dinosaur support collaborative editing?" (Feature exploration)

### Discussion Template

**Title:** Your question or idea, phrased conversationally.
- ✅ "How do other creatives organize their reference boards?"
- ✅ "Idea: Quick-save to specific boards with keyboard shortcut"
- ❌ "FEATURE REQUEST URGENTLY NEEDED"

**Body:**

```
**What I'm thinking about:**
Brief context. Why does this matter to you?

**Current workflow:**
How do you work around this now?

**The question / idea:**
What are you curious about or proposing?

**Example:**
Show a concrete example if helpful.
```

### Good Discussion Practices

- **Be conversational** — Discussions aren't formal; they're explorations
- **Listen to others** — Someone might have a different perspective worth hearing
- **Avoid demands** — "This should be built" vs. "I'd love to see..." reads differently
- **Link to related issues** — Helps others connect ideas
- **React with emojis** — Quick way to show support without cluttering comments

---

## Feature Requests (In Discussions)

When you have a feature idea, start in **Discussions** first. Here's why:

1. Get feedback from other creatives
2. Refine the idea together
3. Surface the best ideas to the team organically

Once a feature is well-discussed and has community support, it can graduate to an Issue or GitHub milestone.

### Feature Discussion Template

**Title:** What's the feature, in user language?
- ✅ "Drag-and-drop organizing cards into groups"
- ❌ "Add grouping state management"

**Body:**

```
**Problem it solves:**
What workflow does this unlock?

**How I'd use it:**
A realistic scenario from your creative practice.

**Why Dinosaur?**
Why does Dinosaur specifically need this?

**Alternatives:**
How do you work around it now?
```

## Community Guidelines

### Be Kind

Dinosaur is built for creatives by creatives. We're all figuring this out together.

### Stay On Topic

- Issues → bugs and confirmed problems
- Discussions → ideas, questions, and feedback
- Off-topic posts get politely redirected

### No Spam, Promotions, or Self-Promotion

Don't post links to unrelated products, services, or your own tools unless directly relevant to the conversation.

### Respect Privacy

Don't share others' personal information, screenshots with identifying details, or private conversations without permission.

### Report Abuse

See something inappropriate? Reach out to the maintainers directly.

## What Happens After You Post

### Issues

1. **Acknowledgment** — We'll say thanks and triage
2. **Reproduction** — We'll try to reproduce the bug
3. **Discussion** — If we need more info, we'll ask
4. **Fix or Workaround** — Once fixed, it goes into the next release

### Discussions

1. **Community Response** — Others may chime in with ideas
2. **Team Review** — We check periodically for patterns
3. **Graduation** — Popular discussions might become formal requests or features

---

## Tips for Getting Your Issue Fixed Faster

1. **Reproduce it yourself, clearly** — Reproducible issues get fixed first
2. **Keep it short** — Respect people's time
3. **Add media** — Screenshots/videos make everything clearer
4. **Comment with updates** — "Still happening in 1.0.0-beta.2" keeps it on our radar
5. **Try workarounds** — Let us know how you worked around it; sometimes that's a clue

---

## Tips for Getting Your Idea Adopted

1. **Show the problem you're solving** — Not just the feature
2. **Link to similar tools** — Show how others handle this
3. **Be specific about your workflow** — Generic ideas are hard to prioritize
4. **Engage in discussion** — Answer questions, refine based on feedback
5. **Check if it's a plugin or extension first** — Some ideas can be built externally
